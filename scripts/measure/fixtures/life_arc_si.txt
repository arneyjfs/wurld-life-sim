The saga defines: the central conflict, the antagonist, factions, key archetypes, broad story movements, the midpoint twist, the climax, candidate endings, and any love-interest fate. This chapter MUST advance the saga, but YOU pick the dramatic question — there's no pre-allocated chapter synopsis.

The player's choices steer the path; a good premise survives the player going off-script (the scene generator + replan absorb divergence). Lean on completed-chapter memories for continuity.
